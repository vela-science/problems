# Inspiration board

A working mood board for Constellate (Canopus, Vela, the essays). The goal
is world-class within our own lane: warm editorial restraint, scientific
diagrams as first-class language, gold as the single accent. Not SaaS, not
dark-neon AI, not a pitch deck.

North star reference (Will): **ivo.ai**. Warm greige ground, a large light
serif display set low and left, one olive accent, type rendered as art,
generous quiet. Premium intelligence with a soul.

---

## 1. Closest to our own DNA (steal directly)

- **Arc Institute** — https://arcinstitute.org/
  Serif indigo statement headline beside a living scientific viz: a DNA helix
  drawn as a navy *constellation of dots* with a gold ribbon. Mono metadata
  chips (`03.2026`, lab tags). Editorial research list. This is Constellate's
  cousin. Steal: lead with a confident serif claim next to a living asterism;
  use mono as texture, not decoration.

- **Works in Progress** — https://worksinprogress.co/
  Cream ground, mono + serif pairing, a dramatic dark scientific image as the
  hero with a mustard caption slab overlapping it, mono category tags. Steal:
  a single scientific image can BE the hero; a colored caption block + mono
  tags give it editorial authority.

- **Stripe Press** — https://press.stripe.com/  (blocked in-browser; known)
  Warm paper, serif, object-led. Each book is a beautiful 3D object on cream.
  "Ideas for progress." Steal: treat every artifact (a plate, the atlas, a
  finding) as a crafted object resting on paper.

- **Asimov Press** — https://press.asimov.com/
  Restrained serif publishing, custom wordmark ligature, pill category tags,
  one dark inset band for the subscribe moment ("Richly storied. Always free.
  No ads."). Steal: editorial calm plus a single dark band for contrast.

## 2. Warm editorial intelligence (the ivo.ai peer set)

- **ivo.ai** — https://www.ivo.ai/
  Warm greige + cream, serif display low-left, olive-green accent, ASCII
  type-art inside rounded olive panels. Steal: warm neutral that is not white
  and not beige; one organic accent; type as image.

- **Anthropic** — https://www.anthropic.com/
  Warm oat ground, serif statements placed asymmetrically (right of center),
  large rounded dark feature panels with a giant faint serif title emerging.
  Steal: asymmetric serif statements; a designed dark panel instead of an
  empty dark void.

- **Sublime** — https://sublime.app/
  Enormous grotesque headline + one soulful photoreal natural object (a mossy
  rock garden) + a butterfly with a tiny file-label tooltip. Steal: a single
  surreal hero object, a poetic line, one wink of delight.

- **Legora** — https://legora.com/
  Cinematic full-bleed golden-hour video, spaced serif wordmark, one
  forest-green accent. Steal: atmosphere can carry a hero if the type stays
  quiet and the palette stays warm.

## What this means for Canopus

The current Canopus hero is a centered stack (kicker, giant centered title,
centered subtitle, two centered buttons), which reads as a template. Vela
already does better with an authored, asymmetric hero and concrete proof.
The board points one direction:

1. **Asymmetric authored hero** (Arc + ivo): a confident serif claim set
   left, the living constellation / real atlas to the right, concrete proof
   beneath (live finding count, repo, version, a plain value prop) as Vela does.
2. **Say it plainly first** (Future House), then earn one resonant line.
3. **Objects on paper** (Stripe Press, Works in Progress): the plates and the
   atlas as crafted objects with a colored caption slab and mono metadata.
4. **Kill the voids**: tighten the long scroll; sections breathe by intent,
   not by accident.
5. **Designed dark, not empty dark** (Anthropic): the night/problem act as a
   composed panel, not a sparse field.
6. **One living detail** (ivo, Sublime): a breathing asterism, a quiet hover,
   instead of heavy scrollytelling everywhere.
