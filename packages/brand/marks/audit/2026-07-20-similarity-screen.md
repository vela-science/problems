# Vela sail visual similarity screen

Date: 2026-07-20

Status: non-legal visual screen. This is not trademark clearance or a legal
opinion. A qualified trademark professional must assess registrability and the
scope of relevant goods, services, and territories before filing or relying on
the mark.

## Selected mark

The product mark remains the original Vela sail released before this audit. Its
canonical full-size geometry is the exact `v0.300.2` sail: two filled state and
lineage fields, a vertical mast, and the Stardust substrate line. The 2026-07-20
optical reinterpretation was rejected and removed; it is not a Vela asset and
must not be restored, shipped, or used as the basis for future exports.

The canonical source is `../source/vela-symbol-full.svg`. Compact, micro,
favicon, lockup, monochrome, reversed, raster, and print files are delivery
variants of the same identity. Their content hashes are recorded in
`../exports/MANIFEST.json`.

## Sources checked

- EUIPO/EUIPN TMview text search for `VELA` across participating offices.
- EUIPO/EUIPN TMview image similarity search using a Vela sail rendering.
- WIPO Global Brand Database availability and image-search guidance.
- General public image results for Vela, sail, research, data, and direction
  marks.

## Finding and disposition

TMview image search surfaced Swiss figurative registration 458278 (application
07704/1998) as a relevant visual comparison. At the time of the screen it was
reported active in Nice classes 16, 41, and 42, with an expiry date of
2028-09-18. Its curved filled sail, vertical stroke, and baseline create a
meaningful similarity concern.

This screen therefore does **not** clear the Vela sail. The product keeps the
original sail as the user-selected identity, but the mark remains provisional
for trademark purposes until qualified review covers the intended territories
and services. No alternate logo is implied or authorized by this document.

No screenshot of the rejected 2026-07-20 reinterpretation is retained in the
active brand package. Historical Git objects remain the audit record.
