# Vela Voice and Messaging

## Category claim
Vela is the scientific state substrate.

## Primary line
Capture state. Compound knowledge. Turn it into direction.

## Definition
Scientific state is the structured, versioned representation of claims, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, dependencies, methods, and history required to continue scientific work.

## Voice
- Precise: concrete nouns, explicit mechanisms, bounded claims.
- Ambitious: speak at the level of infrastructure and inheritance.
- Humble: represent uncertainty; do not overclaim.
- Enduring: prefer durable language over launch-cycle hype.
- Directional: show what becomes possible next.

## Preferred example
Vela records claims, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and dependencies as durable scientific state, so people and agents can continue work without losing context.

## Avoid
Vela revolutionizes discovery with a magical AI-powered knowledge ecosystem.

## Vocabulary
Prefer: state, finding, evidence, provenance, lineage, uncertainty, dependency, repository, receipt, inheritance, direction, substrate.
Avoid: magic, disruption, autonomous scientist, revolutionary, infinite intelligence, one-click science, knowledge graph as an empty slogan.
