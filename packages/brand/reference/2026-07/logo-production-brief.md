# Vela Logo Production Brief

## Objective
Create an irreducible symbol that reads, in order, as:
1. scientific state becoming directional;
2. compounding knowledge and inherited lineage;
3. a subtle V;
4. a sail or vessel.

## Required geometry
- A restrained baseline representing substrate and provenance.
- One dominant convex growth curve.
- One or two internal lineage trajectories, depending on optical size.
- A terminal vertical or near-vertical boundary that can read as axis, frontier, constraint, and mast.
- Negative space that suggests a V without drawing a literal letter.

## Optical masters
- Full: 32 px and above, two lineage trajectories permitted.
- Compact: 20-31 px, one lineage trajectory.
- Micro: 12-19 px, simplified outer curve, axis, and baseline.
- Favicon: hand-hinted on a 16 px pixel grid.

## Non-negotiable tests
- Works in one color.
- Recognizable without the wordmark.
- Does not require a star, glow, gradient, or metallic effect.
- Does not read primarily as yachting, finance, hospitality, or aerospace.
- Does not collapse at 16 px.
- Does not resemble a stock sail icon or an e/acc derivative.

## Wordmark
Custom-draw VELA. Do not use a stock font as final artwork. The V angle, A apex/crossbar, terminals, and optical spacing should derive from the symbol.

## Deliverables
AI/SVG/PDF/EPS masters, outlined and live-type variants, monochrome/reversed versions, lockups, pixel-hinted favicons, construction sheet, clear-space sheet, and visual-similarity audit.
